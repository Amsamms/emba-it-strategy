# Research Notes (with APA sources)

All figures below are pulled from live web research (June 2026). Used to ground Assignment 1 (banking sector) and Assignment 2 (NBE).

## A. Banking sector, global, DECLINING roles and physical footprint
- US bank teller employment declined nearly 30% since 2010; job postings down almost two-thirds (Burning Glass Institute).
- US Bureau of Labor Statistics: teller employment projected to decline 13% from 2024 to 2034 (BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook).
- US bank branches: about 100,000 in 2009, about 77,500 at the start of 2024, falling to below 65,000 by Q2 2025. Roughly 944 branches closed through Q3 2024, with 439 in Q3 2024 alone (S&P Global Market Intelligence; FDIC BankFind).
- UK banks closed more than 450 branches in Q1 2025, continuing a five-year decline (CoinLaw).
- AI chatbots and virtual agents now handle up to 65% of routine banking transactions globally (CoinLaw).
- WEF Future of Jobs 2025: bank tellers and data-entry clerks are among the fastest-declining roles; cashiers, administrative assistants, accountants and auditors also decline sharply (World Economic Forum).

## B. Banking sector, global, EMERGING roles and the net-jobs argument
- WEF: fintech engineers, big-data specialists, and AI/machine-learning specialists are among the fastest-growing jobs to 2030 (World Economic Forum).
- WEF: an estimated 170 million new jobs created 2025-2030, 92 million displaced, net gain of 78 million. AI and data processing alone create about 11 million roles and replace about 9 million (World Economic Forum).
- WEF: 39% of workers' existing skill sets expected to be transformed or outdated over 2025-2030 (World Economic Forum).
- Emerging banking roles in demand: RPA developer / process analyst / automation consultant; cybersecurity analyst / penetration tester / CISO; data scientist / data engineer; cloud engineer; regtech and compliance-tech specialists (SRMSB; Built In; Indeed).
- High-frequency / algorithmic trading accounts for around 70% of US equity market volume (World Economic Forum).

## C. Egypt context (for the Egypt lens)
- Financial inclusion reached 76.3% in June 2025, up from 27% in 2016 (growth above 214%); about 52 million active transaction accounts at end-2024 (74.8%) (Daily News Egypt; Zawya).
- Online and mobile banking: about 18 million subscribers and 114 million transactions worth EGP 11.7 trillion in 2024 (Central Bank of Egypt data via news).
- Meeza national card: more than 43.5 million cards issued by June 2025 (launched 2019) (Transfi; AfricanEnda).
- InstaPay: more than 16 million users and over 1.1 billion transactions worth EGP 2.4 trillion by June 2025 (launched 2022, backed by the Central Bank of Egypt) (Business Tech News / CBE).
- Mobile wallets: 55.5 million e-wallets, 1.4 billion transactions worth over EGP 1.8 trillion by June 2025 (CBE data via news).
- Apple Pay launched December 2024; over 40 million transactions worth more than EGP 32 billion by June 2025 (CBE data via news).
- SME financing portfolios grew by 395% over the inclusion period (CBE data via news).

## D. NBE (National Bank of Egypt) specifics, for Assignment 2
Corporate profile and strategy:
- Founded 1898. Egypt's largest bank by assets, deposits, loans, capital, branches, and employees; ranks 3rd among Arab banks (banksegypt.com; NBE).
- Total assets EGP 8.13 trillion at end-December 2024, up from EGP 5.23 trillion a year earlier (NBE financial position statement).
- Net profits leapt to about $2.76 billion in 2024; H1 2025 profits EGP 166.9 billion, about $3.53 billion (Zawya; Daily News Egypt).
- Network of about 665 branches, offices, and banking units serving nearly 20 million customers; about 30,000 employees (NBE; BIO-key / StockTitan).
- Strategic role: financing the national economy and mega-projects, leading financial inclusion, sustainability (EESG framework and Climate Finance Strategy aligned with IFRS S2; finances solar irrigation and recycling) (Climate Lens Advisors; NBE Responsible Banking).

IT and digital practices:
- Core systems: Oracle Flexcube core banking (2016), Oracle E-Business Suite ERP Financials (2014), Oracle HRMS (2013), R3 Corda blockchain platform (2018), SecPod SanerNow patch management (2023) (AppsRunTheWorld).
- Digital ecosystem: NBE Mobile app, Al Ahly Net internet banking, NBE PhoneCash e-wallet, with fund transfers and bill payments via Fawry (Zawya; MenaFN).
- Mastercard partnership (September 2024): AI-powered digital employees and digital humans; NBE is the first bank in the Middle East to integrate digital humans and augmented reality into banking (Mastercard newsroom).
- Drove the end-to-end architecture for the Apple Pay launch in Egypt in 2024 (CIO; news).
- Fintech and inclusion arms: Al Ahly Momken e-payments (acquired with an EGP 150 million commitment, target EGP 1 billion monthly volume, 40,000 POS); Al Ahly Tamkeen microfinance serving more than 100,000 borrowers (The Startup Scene; Daily News Egypt).
- Cybersecurity: deployed BIO-key PortalGuard identity and access management with MFA and single sign-on for 30,000 employees (rollout by Raya IT), plus a Security Operations Center with advanced risk analysis (Retail Banker International; StockTitan).

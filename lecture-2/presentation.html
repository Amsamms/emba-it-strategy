01 / FOUNDATIONS

Data as a strategic resource

Data resource management is a managerial activity: it applies IS technologies to manage data as a valuable resource that serves the information needs of business stakeholders.

💎

Data is an asset

Well-managed data is a competitive resource, not just a by-product of operations.

📊

Better decisions

Clean, integrated data feeds analytics, reporting, and decision support.

🧮

One version of truth

Shared databases remove redundancy and conflicting records across the business.

🛡

Control & trust

Managed data improves security, integrity, privacy, and compliance.

05 / STRUCTURES

Evaluating the structures

Each structure trades off flexibility, speed, and the kinds of questions it can answer.

StructureStrengthsLimitations
Hierarchical+ Fast for structured, routine transactions− Cannot handle many-to-many relationships
Network+ More flexible than hierarchical− Struggles with ad hoc requests
Relational+ Easy ad hoc queries; easy to maintain− Less fast than hierarchical or network
Multidimensional+ Excellent for OLAP and fast analytics− Specialized; built for analysis, not transactions
Object-Oriented+ Handles complex types: graphics, audio, video− More complex to design and query

Slice and dice

Each axis of the cube is a dimension: Product, Region, and Time. Each cell holds a measure, like total sales. Managers rotate and slice the cube to answer questions fast, with no new query written from scratch.

Tip: slicing by Time = Q1 turns the 3D cube into a flat table of Product by Region for that quarter.

12 / REAL WORLD

Opening the data vault

When data is a managed resource, it can become a platform. These firms opened parts of their databases to outside developers.

🛒

Amazon

Spent ~10 years and $1bn building its data vault. Opened it in 2002; 65,000+ developers and businesses tapped in, many becoming partners.

🌐

eBay

Opened its $3bn databases in 2003. 15,000+ developers registered, 1,000+ apps appeared, and 41% of listings were uploaded using them.

🔍

Google

Moved cautiously: 1,000 free search results a day, more by permission. Opened AdWords to outside apps in 2005.

Discussion: should every company open its databases? Weigh new revenue and innovation against losing control of a core asset.

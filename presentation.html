The IT Strategy & Practices

NBE runs an enterprise-grade IT estate that powers its scale and its digital ambition, organized across four domains.

Core Systems

  • Oracle Flexcube core banking with real-time processing
  • Oracle E-Business Suite ERP and HRMS
  • Modernized data and security infrastructure

Digital Channels

  • NBE Mobile app and Al Ahly Net internet banking
  • NBE PhoneCash e-wallet; bill payment via Fawry
  • Nationwide ATM and branch network

Innovation & Partnerships

  • Mastercard AI digital humans (first in the Middle East)
  • Drove the Apple Pay launch in Egypt (2024)
  • Fintech arms: Al Ahly Momken and Al Ahly Tamkeen

Cybersecurity

  • BIO-key PortalGuard identity and access management
  • MFA and single sign-on for 30,000 employees
  • Security Operations Center with risk analytics

Overall verdict: High alignment. IT is a core strategic enabler at NBE, not a back-office support function. Watch-outs: legacy-system integration, data-analytics maturity, cloud adoption, and ESG-aligned IT.

Recommendations to the Board

Six technologies to deepen alignment and extend NBE's leadership, drawn from the IT Strategy course.

Generative AI

Advanced Analytics

Use big-data analytics and hyper-personalization to grow share of wallet.

Cloud Adoption

Migrate suitable workloads to the cloud for scalability and lower cost.

Open Banking & APIs

Open secure APIs to enable embedded finance and a fintech ecosystem.

Adopt zero-trust architecture and AI-based real-time fraud detection.

Use green data centers and ESG data platforms for sustainability reporting.
